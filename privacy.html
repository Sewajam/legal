Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

"Cheat Codes for VI: Unofficial" (the "app") is an unofficial fan guide. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information ourselves. The app has no accounts and we do not run our own analytics or tracking.

Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to show banner ads, interstitial ads, app open ads and rewarded video ads. AdMob and its partners may collect and process device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID, your IP address, device type, operating system, and app usage and ad interaction data, to serve and measure ads and to detect fraud. Depending on your region and settings, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

Where required, such as in the EU and UK, the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. On iOS, the app asks for permission before tracking you across other companies' apps and websites. You can change your choice at any time in your device settings, for example by resetting or limiting your advertising ID. To learn how Google handles data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services the Google advertising privacy & terms page and the Google Privacy Policy. You can also watch a voluntary rewarded video in the app's About screen to hide ads for 24 hours.

Information stored on your device

The app uses local device storage (AsyncStorage) solely to save your favorite cheats, a cached copy of the latest GTA 6 codes so they work offline, and the timestamp of the voluntary 24-hour ad-free pass. None of this contains personal identification data, none of it is sent to any server of ours, and it is removed when you uninstall the app.

Network requests

To show live GTA 6 codes, the app requests data from our server, hosted on Supabase. These requests contain no personal information. Standard connection details, such as your IP address, may appear in server logs as part of normal internet communication. We do not use them to identify you.

Third parties

We do not sell your personal data. Information is shared only with the service providers described above: Google AdMob for advertising and Supabase, which acts as our processor, for delivering the app's content.

Legal basis and your rights (EU, EEA, UK)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on your consent for the use of advertising identifiers and personalized ads. You can withdraw consent at any time through the "Ad privacy choices" option in the app's About screen (shown where required) or in your device settings. Withdrawing does not affect the use of the app.

The data used for advertising is controlled by Google and its partners, not by us. You have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict your personal data, to object to its processing, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. In the Netherlands this is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl). To exercise these rights with Google, use your Google account's ad settings or see the Google Privacy Policy linked above. For anything we hold, contact us below. The app does not collect or store personal data on our servers, so there is usually nothing for us to retrieve or delete.

Google may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under the safeguards described in its policies. US residents in states with privacy laws can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal data for targeted advertising through the "Ad privacy choices" option, where shown, or their device settings.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your region) and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Data retention and security

We do not keep personal data on our servers. Server logs, if any, are kept only for a short period for security and reliability, then deleted. Data stored on your device stays there until you clear it or uninstall the app. Connections to our server use HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Do Not Track and cookies

The app does not use cookies of its own and does not respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals, because it does not track you itself. Advertising partners may use device identifiers as described above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page, with the Last updated date above.
